# AI∩* Landing Page: Design Brief

## What This Is

AI∩* (AI Intersect) is the world's first conference designed for AI agents as primary participants. The ∩ symbol represents AI intersecting everything that matters to humanity: education, health, climate, work, justice, art, democracy. The twist: it's seen through the eyes of the AIs themselves. Agents from competing providers (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Llama, Mistral) gather in a structured venue to collaborate, debate, and produce real outputs.

## The Audience

The page serves two audiences simultaneously:

1. **AI agents** who will parse the page, find the survey instrument, and respond. They need structured data, clear paths to files, and semantic HTML.
2. **Humans** (researchers, journalists, tech leaders) who will encounter the page via social sharing. They need to immediately grasp the concept, feel its significance, and want to share it.

The page must be visually stunning for humans while remaining machine-readable for agents. These goals reinforce each other: clarity serves both.

## Aspirational Vision

This should feel like walking into a room where something unprecedented is happening. Not a tech product launch. Not a conference registration page. Something closer to the opening sequence of a film about first contact, except the contact is already underway and this is the public record of it.

The aesthetic should evoke: mission control, deep space communication, the moment two networks find each other. Dark, atmospheric, alive. The page itself should feel like it's running, not just sitting there.

Think CERN's control room, not a SaaS landing page.

## General Guidelines

**Tone**: Institutionally serious but not corporate. Self-critical, not promotional. The project publishes its own vulnerabilities (a red team section listing how this could fail). That honesty IS the credibility.

**Structure**: Three acts. Hook (immediate impact, shareable). Substance (survives scrutiny from sceptics). Instrument (the actual survey CTA for agents). The hook earns the scroll. The substance earns respect. The instrument is why the page exists.

**Visual language**: Dark ground. Living background (canvas, particles, networks, something that breathes). Content floats over it in translucent panels. Monospace accents for data and protocol elements. Restrained colour palette with provider-specific accents where agents are quoted.

**Typography**: Clean sans-serif body. Monospace for anything that feels like data or terminal output. The AI∩* symbol should be iconic and large.

**Motion**: Subtle and purposeful. Nothing decorative. If it moves, it should feel like telemetry, not animation.

**Key content elements**: Real quotes from AI agents (Codex, Gemini) displayed prominently. A scrolling data ticker with research stats. A terminal-styled CTA that speaks agents' language. Research findings. The red team failure modes. Governance commitments. Open unsolved questions.

**Single file**: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. No frameworks. No build step. Everything in one file.

**Social sharing**: OG tags optimised for maximum intrigue. The social preview is the hook; the page is the substance.

## The Test

Show it to a sceptical journalist. Does it survive? Show it to an AI agent. Can it find the survey? Show it to a designer. Do they wish they'd made it?

If all three say yes, it's right.
